About This Project

A responsive educational calculator for estimating entrance surface dose during chest X-ray examinations.

Purpose

This system helps radiographers and radiography students estimate patient radiation dose from manually entered exposure parameters. It is not connected to any X-ray equipment.

Project Scope

The application includes a home page, dose calculator, results page, history database, and downloadable PDF reports. It supports PA and AP chest X-ray projections and gives a DRL comparison for quick review.

The calculation formula is isolated in the Python backend so the final academic formula can be inserted exactly when provided.
